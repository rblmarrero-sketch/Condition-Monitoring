HOW TO ADD PHOTOS  (no Excel needed)
====================================

Layout:   <Truck>/<Date>/<Position>.jpg

Example:
    Photos/
      TK146/
        2026-07-29/        <-- an inspection (date = YYYY-MM-DD)
          4C.jpg           <-- one photo per plug position: 4C 4D 4E 4F
          4D.jpg
          4E.jpg
          4F.jpg
        2026-10-15/        <-- add a NEW date folder = a new inspection
          4C.jpg ...
      TK147/
        2026-07-29/ ...

To view:
  1. Open  dashboard/index.html  in Chrome or Edge (double-click the file).
  2. Click  "Open photo folder"  and choose this Photos folder
     (this can be a folder on your N: drive).
  3. Pick a truck to see its history. Click a photo to enlarge.

Grades (optional):
  - Click a photo in the dashboard and pick A/B/C/D (saved in your browser), OR
  - put the grade in the file name, e.g.  4C_C.jpg  or  TK146_4C_2026-10-15_C.jpg
