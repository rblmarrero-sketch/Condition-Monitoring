Machines this round applies to, against machines it reached, in the period selected above. A finding you never went to look for is not an absence of findings.

Top failure modes (Pareto)

Direct causes

Why those defects occurred, as recorded at inspection.

P-F interval — detection to functional failure

Measured wear against its limit. P is where the part becomes a finding (80% worn); F is condemn. The gap between them is the warning the round buys you — if it is shorter than the inspection interval, the round will miss it.

ISO 14224 failure mechanism

Table B.2 mechanism classes — comparable across sites and benchmarks.

Wear and remaining life

Every measured position, latest reading first. Life is projected from this position's own history — two readings at least, and the app says so when it will not guess.

Action register

Every finding that needs work, worst first. Give it an owner and a date, and it stops being a list and starts being a plan.

Inspection history

The programme, honestly counted

How much of the fleet is audited, verified and standardised — against the register, not against the machines somebody happened to visit. Every number states its denominator, because those two get mistaken for each other and that is how a programme reports 90% and means 90% of eleven machines.

Deciding the standard

Not product by product — requirement by requirement. Machines that need the same thing are one decision, however many badges are on them. Choose, and the matrix and the posters re-read.

The 2027 shelf against the recommendations

Specification 02 rev CO-05 is what the site may actually buy. The fleet matrices say what should go in each compartment. Where the two do not line up, somebody has either told a fitter to use an oil nobody ordered, or ordered an oil for nothing — and the season is a third answer neither of them can show.

Machine reference

Capacities, specifications and intervals, per model. A capacity is the one number here that causes a failure on its own — an under-filled final drive seizes, an over-filled one blows a seal — so changing it asks where the figure came from.

Fleet matrix

Every asset class on one sheet — compartment, capacity, interval, and the oil that belongs in it. Colour is identity and nothing else: which product, never how good it is.

Shop posters

The sheet that goes on the workshop wall. A standard that lives only in a database is not a standard — this is the version a fitter reads at arm's length with a head-torch, so it is bilingual, it is large, and it says what to do when the right drum is empty.
